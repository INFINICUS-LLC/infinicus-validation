Privacy Policy

This policy describes INFINICUS's privacy practices as the platform actually operates today. "INFINICUS," "we" and "our" refer to the operator to be verified in Entity Status; the entity name does not change what data is actually collected, described below.

Information and purposes

  • Account data — when you create an account, your email address and authentication credentials are collected and stored by Supabase, our authentication and database provider, on our behalf. We do not see or store your raw password.
  • Business and integration data — if you use Business Operations, Business Digital Twin, AI Decision Intelligence, Approved Business Action, Outcome Monitoring, Data Acquisition, or Continuous Learning, the records you enter (sales, expenses, inventory, customer and team events, data sources, approved decisions, and recorded outcomes) are stored server-side in our own database, associated with your account, so they persist across devices and sessions. Depending on the source, this may include information about your own customers, staff or other people — you are responsible for having authority to provide it.
  • Simulation Engine inputs — the standalone Simulation Engine (business idea, capital, price, industry, location) runs its Monte Carlo calculations in your browser. Simulation history saved for a signed-in account is stored server-side; simulation history for a signed-out visitor is kept only in that browser's localStorage.
  • AI inputs and outputs — business descriptions, simulation metrics, and live business-platform data you choose to analyze are sent to Anthropic's API to generate AI narrative analysis and AI Decision Intelligence recommendations. This is a currently enabled, confirmed data flow — not a hypothetical one. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Technical information — requests, IP addresses, device/browser information, and diagnostic events are processed by our hosting, CDN and error-monitoring services (Cloudflare, Hetzner, Sentry — see Providers & Subprocessors) for delivery, security, troubleshooting and reliability.
  • Advertising/analytics cookies — if you accept this page's cookie banner, we set consent-mode cookies that enable Google ad-performance measurement and analytics (see Cookies & Browser Storage).
  • Browser storage — UI preferences, cached labels, feature-seen flags, your cookie-consent choice, and — for signed-out visitors — Simulation Engine history are held in your browser's localStorage.

Service operation and learning

Business data supports the Business Operations, BI/KPI, Digital Twin, simulation, AI Decision Intelligence and Outcome Monitoring workflows described above. "Continuous learning" refers to feedback and evaluation features; it does not itself authorize training general-purpose AI models on your data. Any use for model training or a materially different purpose would require a separately disclosed basis and appropriate terms. No AI-provider training or retention guarantee is made here — confirm Anthropic's current terms directly.

Roles and lawful processing

For account administration and our own service operations, the verified operator acts as a controller. When we process personal data for a business customer's purposes and instructions, roles must be defined in the applicable DPA. Business customers are responsible for having authority to provide data, give instructions and connect third-party systems. Contract performance, legitimate interests, consent and legal obligations may be the relevant basis depending on the activity and your jurisdiction; this draft does not assert that consent alone covers every activity.

Recipients and international processing

Information is made available to the confirmed service providers listed in Providers & Subprocessors, to integrations you authorize, or as required by law. Any corporate-transaction disclosure would require appropriate safeguards. Exact hosting regions, recipient legal entities and cross-border transfer safeguards for Hetzner and Supabase have not been verified for this edition; this page makes no U.S.-only, EU-only or other residency promise. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties; whether any ad-sharing arrangement would count as a "sale" or "share" under laws like the CCPA still needs legal confirmation.

Retention and deletion

No fixed retention period is established by this draft. The final policy must specify retention periods or criteria for account data, business records, logs, diagnostics, backups and provider-held information. Clearing your browser's local storage affects that device only — it does not delete server-side account or business data. Account closure and server-side deletion require contacting us at the channel in Contact, once verified.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Mandatory statutory rights and response periods remain applicable regardless of this draft. The INFINICUS privacy request channel is not yet verified; it must be published in Contact before this notice takes effect.

Cookies & Browser Storage

On your first visit anywhere on the site, including this page, a cookie banner asks you to accept or decline advertising/analytics cookies. If you accept, we and Google may use cookies to measure ad performance and site usage. If you decline, only essential browser storage and security cookies are used.

What this site actually loads

This Legal Center page loads the same scripts as the rest of the platform: a consent-gated Google advertising/analytics tag, Sentry error monitoring, and Google Fonts. None of these are hidden from this page — a claim that any INFINICUS page loads no trackers would be inaccurate and is not made here.

  • Essential storage — Supabase sets a session token (cookie or local storage, depending on sign-in method) so you stay signed in between visits. Cloudflare may set a __cf_bm cookie for bot management; this is a security cookie with no advertising purpose.
  • Functional storage (localStorage) — your cookie-consent choice, UI preferences, cached labels, feature-seen flags, and signed-out Simulation Engine history. Not a cookie; kept in your browser only.
  • sessionStorage — temporary event data, cleared when you close the tab.
  • Optional — consent-mode cookies (only if you click Accept) — enable Google ad-performance measurement and analytics (ad storage, analytics storage, ad user data, ad personalization). Never set if you click Decline.
  • Error monitoring (Sentry) — loaded on page view to capture errors and performance traces for reliability; this is not gated by the cookie banner because it does not set advertising cookies, but it does transmit diagnostic data (see Providers & Subprocessors).

Third-Party Cookies

  • Google (Ads/Analytics) — if you accept the banner, Google may set cookies for ad-performance measurement and analytics. See Google's cookie policy.
  • Google Fonts — may set cookies for font caching; functional and non-tracking.
  • Cloudflare — the __cf_bm security cookie described above.

Your Choices

You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, which shows the consent banner again on your next visit. Declining does not affect your ability to create an account or use the Service — it only disables ad-performance/analytics cookies. Clearing all localStorage resets signed-out Simulation Engine history and UI preferences but does not delete your account or server-stored business data.
