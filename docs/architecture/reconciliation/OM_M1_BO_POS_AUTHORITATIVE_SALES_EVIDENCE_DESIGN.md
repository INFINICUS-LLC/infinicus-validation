# M1 — BO/POS authoritative sales evidence contract (design only)

Baseline: `main` at `b0d4abe` (disabled OM Owner Policy v1 merged as #331). Status: **proposal for BO/POS and owner approval**. No migration, POS writer, metric, verdict, CL release or deployment is authorized by this document.

## Existing authority and concrete gaps

| Record and writer | Existing guarantee | Gap for `NET_SALES_REVENUE_V1` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `platform.orders`, `OrderRepository.complete` | BO/POS serializes a planned order and in one transaction sets completed, writes applied tender, `order_events(completed)` and `business_events(sale)`. `void` changes only a planned order to failed. | `order_date` is creation date; total/status are mutable. No immutable amount/currency snapshot or database-enforced unique completion event. No completed-order reversal writer. |
| `business_operations.order_payments` (0203/0206) | Append-only tender rows; deferred check enforces completed order, same currency/register and applied tender sum = order total. | Tender is payment evidence, not a refund or revenue correction. Existing check depends on mutable order total/status. |
| `business_operations.order_events` (0025) | Event ID, scope, order, transition, `occurred_at`, `created_at`, correlation. | Caller-supplied `occurred_at`; UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE and duplicate completed events are not prevented in the database. No exact minor-unit amount, currency or correction link. |
| `business_operations.business_events` (0158) | Same-transaction sale activity row with order ID in `correlation_id`; used by existing KPIs/twin/register views. | Lightweight activity fact, no governed immutable order/currency/amount/status proof. Must not become an alternative sales ledger. |
| `credit_notes` → optional `invoices.order_id` (0026) | Financial adjustment with ID, positive numeric amount, currency and draft/issued/applied/cancelled status. | Status is mutable; invoice/order link optional; `issue_date` is a date, not posted-refund time; no refund tender, posting/reversal/correction event, same-scope/currency composite link or dedup key. An applied credit is not proven money returned. |
| `offline_sales` (0210), `OfflineSaleRepository` | Append-only intake result with `(business_id, client_sale_id)` uniqueness, request digest, device and received times; accepted requests complete through the same `OrderRepository`. | Device time is a claim and offline receipt is not another sale. Needs one canonical completed event keyed by resulting order; delayed intake timing must be defined. |
| `SalesFactsRepository` / M1A / M2A readers | BO-derived daily figures by order creation date; read-only per-sale and window diagnostics expose missing or mutable evidence. | Current “net” is after discounts, not refunds. Readers are explicitly nonauthoritative and cannot fill source gaps. |

Online mutation routes already use `Idempotency-Key`; offline intake has its own client sale ID. Both should be retained. A database uniqueness key is still needed for every economic event, including non-HTTP and retry paths.

## Proposed BO event contract

**Identity and ownership.** BO is the only writer of sale completion, refund posting and corrections. POS calls BO services; DAL may supply validated source provenance but cannot decide or post a sale/refund. BI may derive values from BO facts. OM receives only a later approved read port and cannot mutate BO. No new independent sales ledger is proposed.

Each immutable economic event must carry: BO event UUID; tenant/workspace/business IDs; original `order_id` and, for refund, `credit_note_id` plus original sale-completion event ID; `event_kind` (`SALE_COMPLETED`, `REFUND_POSTED`, `REFUND_REVERSED`, or an explicitly approved correction kind); event/schema version; currency code and signed **integer minor-unit delta**; database-issued UTC `economic_at` and `recorded_at`; authoritative BO writer and authenticated actor/service IDs; source system, source record/event ID and content digest; correlation/idempotency key; optional `reverses_event_id` / `corrects_event_id` with reason. A device-claimed timestamp is provenance only until the owner approves its economic-time treatment. Never accept a client-supplied `recorded_at` or an unsigned amount whose sign must be guessed downstream.

**Completion.** Prefer strengthening the existing `order_events(completed)` source with an immutable completion-specific snapshot and unique `(business_id, order_id)` completion constraint, rather than creating a second sale event. Write it in `OrderRepository.complete`'s existing transaction after all lines/tender rules pass. Snapshot the final order total in exact minor units, currency and applied discount/tax semantics. One completed order contributes one positive sale amount at its BO economic time. Draft/planned, failed/voided and unaccepted offline intents contribute nothing. An existing `business_events(sale)` row remains an activity projection, never a second counted sale.

**Posted refund.** An `applied` credit note is not sufficient. Add a BO-owned append-only posting/reversal evidence relation, referencing the original completed sale and optionally the credit note/invoice, with a mandatory same tenant/workspace/business/currency relationship. A posting is a negative minor-unit delta only after the approved refund settlement/financial-posting point; a reversal is a new positive delta referencing exactly one posting. Partial refunds are permitted; enforce that the active posted total never exceeds the approved refundable sale amount unless a separately approved adjustment policy exists. Draft, issued, cancelled or unposted notes contribute nothing. Require unique source event/idempotency key and unique reversal target. A refund may occur in a different period from its sale; it remains linked to that original sale and is counted at its own authorized economic time.

**Corrections.** Never edit or delete finalized economic events. A correction is a new event referencing exactly one prior event and explaining the signed delta/reversal; enforce same scope and currency, valid sequence, one active reversal per target, and no cycle. Preserve the entire chain and a deterministic source digest. BO decides whether correcting a completed order changes the operational order state or only financial evidence; OM must reread the chain and invalidate any prior measurement digest rather than silently rewriting a past result. Late-arriving changes make affected periods incomplete until remeasured under the approved correction/version rule.

**Deduplication and status.** Database uniqueness must cover one completion per order, `(business_id, source_system, source_event_id, event_kind)` for external or device events, and refund/reversal operation IDs; retries with the same digest return the original result, a reused key with different content conflicts. Enforce order state and same-scope foreign keys inside the database, not only in API code. Use `numeric(18,4)` only at existing BO boundaries with checked ISO minor-unit conversion; store or expose exact signed minor units for the governed event. Unknown currency/exponent and fractional minor units refuse.

## Proposed implementation sequence after owner approval

1. Add scoped composite keys, completion uniqueness, append-only UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE guards, database-issued timestamps and exact amount/currency snapshots to the existing BO completion event path. Decide how legacy events are marked unproven. Review impact on existing POS completion, tender trigger, register/KPI readers and offline intake.
2. Add BO-owned refund posting/reversal tables or an approved extension to the credit-note workflow, with composite order/credit/sale links, uniqueness, RLS, append-only triggers, authorized posting role and audit. Do not repurpose mutable credit-note status as a posted event.
3. Change the BO `OrderRepository.complete` and future refund writer in separate POS-coordinated PRs; retain existing API idempotency and offline replay guarantees. Provide a read-only BO evidence port for BI/OM only after source invariants pass.
4. Disposable PostgreSQL integration and negative tests: completion versus creation date; duplicate/concurrent completion; partial/full and over-refund; cancelled/unposted credit; reversal replay; cross-business/tenant/workspace/currency links and RLS; incorrect minor precision; offline duplicate/clock skew; source correction after measurement; invalid timestamp/order; UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE refusal; crash/rollback atomicity. Compare BI-derived values to BO source IDs without creating another ledger.

No legacy row may be fabricated into an immutable completion or posted refund. A coverage start/watermark and explicit missing-history status are required before any M2 window can be certified.

## Frozen-spec and POS conflicts / owner decisions

The locked BO specification makes native POS an operational interface while BO governs records and permits manager-approved refunds; OM's locked specification forbids bypassing BO or claiming causality without evidence. The proposed ownership matches both. **The current POS first-version decision explicitly excludes refunds, and its offline design keeps refunds online-only.** A refund writer therefore requires a separately authorized POS/BO block; it cannot be smuggled into M1 measurement work. No frozen-spec amendment appears necessary for this design, but changing the meaning of `business_events(sale)`, current BI “net”, or device time would conflict with their current contracts.

Owner rulings required before implementation: (1) whether a credit note can represent an actual posted refund and the precise settlement/posting point; (2) completed-sale correction/reversal state and tax/discount treatment; (3) online versus delayed-offline economic time; (4) legacy-data policy and coverage start; (5) refund/reversal authorization and POS ownership; (6) exact event extension/table shape and migration order. Until these are approved and verified, `NET_SALES_REVENUE_V1` stays nonauthoritative, VERIFIED/FAILED remain disabled and OM → CL learning stays closed.
