# M1-A1F — disabled exact-money comparison harness

Base: `main` at `551ac7c`. Status: **synthetic mathematics only; nonauthoritative**. No jurisdiction, tax rate, exemption, discount treatment, rounding stage, tie rule or UOM precision is approved by this block.

`exactMoneyHarness.ts` accepts bounded signed integer-minor-unit strings and decimal-string quantities. It uses normalized BigInt rationals for intermediate arithmetic, explicit `HALF_AWAY_FROM_ZERO` or `TIES_TO_EVEN` rounding, and refuses unknown currency exponents, unsafe JavaScript number inputs, unsupported quantity precision, division by zero and out-of-bound results. The 38-digit cap is a synthetic harness resource limit, not a database money contract.

`syntheticTaxComparison.ts` compares `LINE_SPECIFIC` and `PROPORTIONAL_LARGEST_REMAINDER` discounts, plus `PER_LINE_PER_RATE` and `AGGREGATE_PER_RATE` tax rounding. The proportional method assigns floor quotas on eligible displayed line values, then residual minor units by descending remainder and ascending stable line ID. It conserves the discount, refuses ineligible/over-capacity allocations and sorts outputs by ID. Taxable, zero-rated and exempt classifications remain distinct. Inclusive tax is extracted from discounted displayed gross; exclusive tax is added to discounted displayed price. The stage and mode are always caller supplied synthetic choices. No engine selects which is legal.

For comparison, extended unit price times quantity is rounded to a line's minor unit using the supplied mode before discount allocation. Other legally possible extended-price rounding stages are outside this harness and remain an owner decision.

Every basket output is `illustrative: true`, `authoritative: false`, `taxPolicyActivated: false`. It reports displayed subtotal, discount, included and exclusive tax, payable, per-line values and rate-group totals. Invariant: `payable = displayed subtotal - discount + exclusive tax`. Included tax is within payable and is never added twice. For aggregate rounding, line tax remains an exact rational and only the rate-group total is rounded; the harness does not manufacture a per-line rounded tax allocation.

Fixed vectors cover USD exclusive 10,000 − 1,000 + 900 = 9,900 cents; inclusive 11,000 − 1,100 = 9,900 with 900 cents embedded tax; mixed 10%/5% rates with 300-cent discount produce 1,828 cents under the supplied per-line half-away option; mixed inclusive/exclusive; zero-rated versus exempt; 0.125 quantity under explicitly supplied UOM precision; one-cent residual; signed negative half-unit adjustment. Two 5-cent lines at 10% demonstrate divergence: per-line half-away tax 2 cents, aggregate half-away 1 cent, per-line ties-even 0 cents, aggregate ties-even 1 cent. These results compare algorithms, not legal tax outcomes.

Focused tests include 500 seeded discount conservation/permutation cases and 200 seeded mixed-basket reconciliation/permutation cases. Manual input mutations of rate, eligibility and rounding method change only illustrative outputs. Existing online/offline `OrderRepository`, `OfflineSaleRepository` and routes have no harness call site; no order, tender, refund, event, database, POS response, OM or CL path changes.
