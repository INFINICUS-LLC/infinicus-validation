# M1-A7 — Offline changed-payable operational approval package

**Status: PROPOSED FOR OWNER DECISION; NO OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY GRANTED.** Baseline: `main` at `e120abf3ac434499159592930b4feeeaef8c140b` (M1-A6-FIX-1 merged, migration ledger through 0218). This package does not amend POS-9/OFF-1, grant permissions, activate tax policy, or authorize a writer, migration, payment, refund, order completion, OM verdict, or CL learning.

## Binding facts and decision boundary

- BO owns order completion. POS-9 D4 permits `accepted_with_difference` for governed **catalog-price drift**; it does not approve a tax-driven change to the customer's confirmed payable. OFF-1 `needs_review` is an immutable intake with no order. Its first response remains the replay answer. Exception resolution belongs to a later OFF-4 contract.
- POS-9 D5 is cash-only offline and excludes offline refunds. D8 requires an owner or administrator with `bo:admin` and a recorded reason to resolve an offline exception. D8 does not itself establish quote, customer-consent, payment, tax-policy or finalization authority.
- The M1-A6 relation accepts only `EVIDENCE_NOTE_RECORDED` and `EVIDENCE_NOTE_SUPERSEDED`. The application role has read-only access after its grant script runs. Its event fields are nonauthoritative claims; a row cannot represent a quote, consent, tender, refund, approval or final sale. No public read or write route exists.
- The current offline intake and order contracts have no tax-aware changed-payable response or review-resolution writer. No jurisdiction-specific policy, legal treatment, customer-confirmation method or production tax activation is approved.

**Decision instruction:** The owner should approve or reject each proposed ruling below separately, identify the approver and policy version, and obtain the listed POS, security, payment and legal/business sign-offs. Silence or a general instruction to continue is not ratification. Approval of a rule authorizes contract amendment preparation only; implementation requires a separately scoped block.

## Contract amendments required before implementation

1. **POS-9 decision record D4/D8 and offline client contract:** state explicitly that tax-driven payable change is outside allowed catalog drift; define customer reauthorization, provisional receipt disclosure and which actor handles review. Preserve D1 native-shell, D5 cash-only, D7 provisional receipt and current online behavior.
2. **OFF-1/OFF-4 API contract:** add a separately versioned review reason, quote/confirmation/finalization commands and response semantics. Keep `POST /offline-sales` first-answer replay and `accepted_with_difference` unchanged. Do not add those commands until the signatures above authorize them.
3. **BO financial and security contracts:** define approved tax-policy source/version, quote expiry, per-transition canonical role mapping, cash reconciliation and retained evidence. R2 and R4 need explicit payment/legal decisions; R3 needs security sign-off. No permission name is invented here.
4. **Schema contract:** distinguish future operational events from the two M1-A6 structural note kinds. Any new event taxonomy, payload, role grant, writer identity or migration needs separate approval and disposable PostgreSQL proof. Existing evidence never silently promotes into approval.
